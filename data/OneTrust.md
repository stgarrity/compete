OneTrust is a leading software company that provides a comprehensive platform for privacy, security, and data governance. Their solutions assist organizations in responsibly collecting, managing, and utilizing data, ensuring compliance with global regulations such as GDPR and CCPA. By offering tools for consent and preference management, third-party risk assessment, and data discovery, OneTrust enables businesses to build trust with consumers and stakeholders. ￼

Founded in 2016, OneTrust has rapidly grown to serve over 14,000 customers worldwide, ranging from large enterprises to small businesses. Their platform integrates AI-powered insights to stay abreast of global regulatory changes, providing a centralized inventory for data management and incident response tools to mitigate risks. This comprehensive approach allows organizations to enforce responsible use of data and AI, aligning with both regulatory requirements and ethical standards. ￼
